welcome to imperfection.

This little corner of the screen isn't for anything in particular — no goals, no scores, nothing to get "right." It's just a soft, colorful world of shapes that react to you, built for letting off steam, relaxing for a minute, or just having a bit of fun with color and motion.

How it works

  • Move your cursor or finger around and watch the lattice ripple beneath the shapes.
  • Tap or click a shape to set it off — triggering dynamic audio feedback that matches your chosen shape style while shapes pop, fling, rainbow, grow, spin, or split.
  • Tap empty space to send a shockwave rippling through everything at once with an atmospheric bass pulse.

The settings (⚙️ top right)

  • Sound toggle (🔊) — mute or unmute audio feedback anytime.
  • Presets — swap the whole mood in one tap: Colorful, Pastel, or Dark-ish.
  • Shape style — switch shapes & audio between bouncy Geometric (funky synth boops), soft Floral (grounded wooden marimba & chimes), or dreamy Sky (ethereal shimmers & cosmic sweeps).
  • Randomize — "Randomize all" reshuffles everything; "Shapes only" keeps your background as is and just remixes the shapes.
  • Background / Pattern / Outline — pick your own colors by hand for the canvas, the wavy pattern, and the shape outlines.
  • Shape colors — the palette your floating shapes are drawn from.

Set it up however feels good, then just let your hands wander.
